Getting started

How do I import my I/O list?
  1. In Excel, open your I/O list and choose File → Save As → CSV (Comma delimited).
  2. Save it somewhere the Files app can reach — iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.
  3. In IO Checkout, tap + → Import I/O List (CSV) and pick the file.
  4. Map each column to a field. The app guesses from your headers; correct anything it got wrong.
  5. Check the preview of the first rows, then tap Import.

Only the Tag column is required. Everything else is optional.

Why can't I import an .xlsx file directly?

Excel workbooks can contain formulas, multiple sheets, merged cells and formatting that have no single correct interpretation as an I/O list. Rather than guess and silently get your data wrong, IO Checkout asks for CSV, which is unambiguous.

In Excel: File → Save As → CSV (Comma delimited). It takes a few seconds and you keep full control over which sheet is used.

My CSV won't import, or the columns look wrong
  • Make sure the first row contains your column headers, and that the First row is headers toggle matches your file.
  • If tags are missing, check that the Tag field is mapped to the right column — it is the only required field.
  • If accented characters look wrong, re-export from Excel as CSV UTF-8.
  • Rows with no tag are skipped, and the app tells you how many.
  • Duplicate tags are imported, not dropped, but you get a warning so you can decide.

If it still won't come in, email us a few sample rows and we will look at it.

I don't have an I/O list handy — can I try it first?

Yes. Go to Settings → Demo → Create Sample Project, or tap Try the Sample Project on the empty screen. That builds a realistic 40-point demo panel — partly checked, with a failed point, an open punch item and a resolved one — so you can walk the whole workflow, report included, without importing anything.

Using it in the field

What is Rapid Mode?

A full-screen, one-point-at-a-time flow for walking a panel with a meter in your other hand. Swipe right to pass, left to fail, or use the large buttons. It advances automatically to the next unchecked point.

Open it from the lightning-bolt button in the Checkout toolbar. Skip sends a point to the back of the queue; Undo reverses the last change.

Does it work with no signal?

Completely. Everything is stored on your device and there is no server, no account and no sign-in. It behaves identically inside a steel enclosure in a basement and at your desk. Importing, checking points, photos, punch list and PDF export all work in airplane mode.

How does the punch list keep history?

Points marked Failed or Punch List collect on the Punch List tab. When you resolve one, you add a note describing the fix — and the original failure, its date, its note and its photos are all preserved.

Your report shows both the problem and the fix, which is what a client actually wants to see. A resolved item can be reopened if it comes back.

Can I add my company logo to reports?

Yes. Settings → Report Branding sets a company logo and a footer line used on every report. An individual project can override the logo in Edit Project Info — useful when you work under more than one brand.

Reports

How do I get the report out of the app?

Open a project, go to the Report tab, capture your signature if you want one on the document, then tap Generate PDF Report. The standard share sheet opens, so you can AirDrop it, email it, or save it to Files.

Export Results CSV on the same screen sends the data back to Excel with statuses, timestamps, initials, notes and punch resolutions intact.

The PDF won't open, or looks damaged

Make sure you are on the current version of the app — an early build could write an empty file. Update from the App Store, generate the report again, and it should open normally.

If a report still won't open, email us and say roughly how many points and photos the project has.

Can the client sign it?

The report ends with a sign-off page. Your signature is captured in the app and printed there; alongside it is a blank witness line for the client to sign by hand on the printed copy.

Trial & purchase

How does the free trial work?

You get a 5-day trial with every feature unlocked. It starts when you accept it on the welcome screen, not before, and nothing is charged automatically — there is no subscription and nothing renews.

When the trial ends, projects you have already created stay on your device and remain fully editable. You will never lose a job in progress. What locks is importing, creating new projects, and exporting PDF or CSV reports.

I bought the app on another device — how do I get it here?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen or in About → Licence. The unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it works on every device you own at no extra cost.

The unlock screen says the price is unavailable

That means the App Store did not return the purchase. Check your network connection and tap Retry. If your device is offline or the App Store is having trouble, it usually clears within a few minutes.

The app never shows a made-up price — if you see no price, it genuinely could not reach the store, so nothing can be charged by mistake.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

If something in the app didn't work as promised, please email us first — we would rather fix it.

Your data

Where is my data stored, and is any of it sent anywhere?

Everything stays on your device. IO Checkout has no account, no server and no analytics, and it does not transmit your projects, photos or reports anywhere. You share a report only when you choose to.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I back up my projects?

Projects are included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so an iCloud or Finder backup captures them. For a copy you control directly, use Export Results CSV and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or your computer.

Deleting the app deletes its projects. Export anything you need to keep before you delete it.
