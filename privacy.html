Privacy Policy

IO Checkout for iPhone and iPad

Effective 9 September 2026 · Applies to all versions of IO Checkout.

IO Checkout collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you create stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. IO Checkout does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to store, lose or hand to anyone else.

The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no registration and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you create is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad and never leaves it unless you deliberately share it:

  • Projects, including client, site and machine details
  • I/O points, statuses, timestamps, initials and notes
  • Photos you attach to points
  • Your company name, logo and inspector details
  • Your captured signature
  • Generated PDF and CSV reports

This data is included in your normal device backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than ours. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access only so you can photograph wiring, terminations or nameplates and attach them to an I/O point. It asks for photo library access only so you can attach an existing photo or choose a company logo for your reports.

Both are used solely when you tap to add a photo. Images are stored on your device with the project and are not uploaded anywhere. The app is fully usable if you deny both permissions.

Sharing a report is your choice

When you export a PDF or CSV, the app hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet and you decide where it goes — AirDrop, Mail, Files or another app you have installed. We are not involved in that transfer and never receive a copy. Once a file leaves the app, it is governed by whatever service you sent it to.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see, receive or store your payment details, billing address or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has purchased the unlock, and receives yes or no.

The free trial is tracked locally on your device using a stored start date. That date is never transmitted anywhere.

Children

IO Checkout is a professional tool for industrial commissioning work and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Since the app collects no data, we do not expect material changes; any that did occur would be described in the App Store release notes as well.
